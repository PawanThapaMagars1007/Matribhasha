# Cultural content guidelines

Matribhasha records knowledge shared by communities. A published record represents a contribution and its review history, not a claim that one version is universal or authoritative.

## Preserve context

Keep the language and variety identified by the contributor. Preserve original wording, transliteration, translations, spelling variants, speaker attribution choice, region and date where approved, and source notes. Do not silently normalize dialect, ritual terminology, names, or performance text. Linguistic review is separate from cultural, permission, and source review.

## Respect sensitive knowledge

Ask the relevant community whether an item can be collected, stored, transcribed, translated, indexed, streamed, or reused. Mark seasonal, sacred, restricted, or otherwise sensitive content. Use restricted or not-publishable access when public circulation is not approved. A content administrator cannot override community restrictions by changing status alone.

## Attribution and corrections

Respect anonymous and pseudonymous choices. Keep submitted versions and review decisions in revision history. Add context or a new version rather than erasing meaningful variants. Contributors and rights holders may request access changes, correction, or withdrawal.
