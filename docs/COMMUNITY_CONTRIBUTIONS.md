# Community contributions

Contributors can submit cultural entries and stories with title, description or original-language text, language, variety, source notes, attribution preference, and permission notes. Community-scoped submissions require approved membership. New submissions remain private and pending review.

Contributors should share only material they are authorized to provide and should describe permission scope in plain language. The intake confirmation records the contributor's assertion; it is not independent proof of consent or ownership. Reviewers must verify permission with the appropriate speaker, rights holder, or community before publication.

Contributor profiles are private by default and may use an account name, pseudonym, or anonymous display when made public. Do not collect caste, ethnicity, religion, or other sensitive identity fields as a condition of contributing.

Submissions can be corrected, withdrawn, or referred for community review. Keep identifying details out of public source notes unless their publication is explicitly approved.
