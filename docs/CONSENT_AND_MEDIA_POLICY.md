# Consent and media policy

Consent must cover the intended act: collection or recording, preservation, transcription, translation, access, and reuse. Record the grantor, scope, date, expiry if any, and person recording the grant. A checkbox is an intake statement, not verification.

Audio is uploaded to private storage, checked against supported file signatures, capped at 10 MB, and held pending review. Playback is allowed only when the audio asset and all linked cultural records permit access. A direct audio URL must not bypass restrictions on linked cultural material.

Image and video entries currently use secure public HTTPS links only. The application does not provide private image/video storage or proxying, so links must not expose material intended to be restricted. Do not publish links until permission, source, and cultural reviews are complete.

Withdrawal requests are reviewed and recorded. An access-change request does not itself change access; a consent withdrawal or takedown request places eligible content on hold while it is reviewed. Approval revokes active grants and hides content. Keep the minimum audit record needed to explain the decision.
