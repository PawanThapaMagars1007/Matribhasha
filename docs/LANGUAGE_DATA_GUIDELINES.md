# Language data guidelines

- Add language records through an authorized administration workflow; never bake a permanent catalog into React.
- Record the community-preferred name, native name, aliases, regional variety, and orthographic conventions with source and contributor attribution.
- Treat language, ethnicity, caste, and identity as distinct concepts.
- A language may have multiple scripts, an evolving writing practice, or an oral tradition. Do not require an alphabet or default to Devanagari.
- Keep original text in Unicode and test grapheme clusters, combining marks, font fallback, search, and copy/paste for every supported script.
- Mark claims with a verification state and reviewer/source. Do not display an unreviewed item as authoritative.
- Store audio provenance, speaker consent, allowed uses, attribution preference, and removal contact. Do not synthesize or label generated speech as native-speaker audio.
- Stroke order, IPA, translation, grammar, and pronunciation feedback must be supplied or reviewed by a qualified language contributor; do not infer these from another language.
- Respect community restrictions on stories, recordings, cultural knowledge, and reuse licenses.
