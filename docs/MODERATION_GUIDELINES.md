# Moderation guidelines

Review dimensions remain separate: linguistic accuracy, cultural/community suitability, permission/consent, and source/provenance. Record the dimension, decision, note, and evidence. A decision in one dimension does not imply approval in another.

Community administrators and cultural reviewers handle community-scoped material according to community authority and access rules. Sacred and restricted material requires approved community authorization before publication.

Review reports for harassment, privacy exposure, misinformation presented as verified, and unauthorized disclosure of restricted knowledge. Use proportionate actions, preserve an audit record, and provide a clear outcome. Contributors may appeal through a community administrator or platform contact.

Popularity or majority vote is not evidence of linguistic or cultural truth. Record disagreement and variation transparently.
